CHEMBASE PRO — MASTER PRODUCT & STARTUP PROMPT

1. PROJECT OVERVIEW

I am building a startup called ChemBase Pro.

ChemBase Pro is intended to become a complete digital ecosystem for Chemical Engineering students, covering their journey from the beginning of their degree through graduation, internships, final-year projects, and preparation for their first engineering job.

The core vision is:

«ChemBase Pro — The digital workspace for Chemical Engineers, from first semester to first job.»

ChemBase Pro should NOT simply be another generic AI chatbot.

Instead, AI should be the intelligence layer that connects specialized Chemical Engineering tools, databases, calculators, simulations, learning resources, laboratory support, process-safety resources, industrial knowledge, academic productivity tools, and career preparation into one platform.

The platform should help students learn, calculate, visualize, practice, simulate, analyze, prepare, and build their careers.

5. AI CHEMICAL ENGINEERING TUTOR

The AI tutor is the central intelligence layer.

It should be specialized in Chemical Engineering rather than behaving like a generic chatbot.

It should be able to:

- Explain concepts
- Solve numerical problems
- Show step-by-step methodology
- Identify relevant equations
- Explain assumptions
- Explain units
- Check dimensional consistency
- Interpret results
- Generate practice problems
- Generate MCQs
- Generate viva questions
- Adjust difficulty according to the student's level
- Explain concepts using industrial examples
- Reference the relevant ChemBase module/tool
- Recommend what the student should study next

For numerical problems, the AI should preferably follow:

Given → Required → Assumptions → Relevant equation → Substitution → Calculation → Units → Final answer → Interpretation

It must NOT simply output an answer.

It should teach the reasoning.
