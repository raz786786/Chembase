FINAL PRODUCT PRINCIPLE
Build Industry around this philosophy:
“Subjects teach the theory.”
“Lab Assistant teaches students how to perform and analyze experiments.”
“Industry teaches students how that Chemical Engineering knowledge is actually used in industrial plants.”
Everything should be interconnected.
For example:
Shell & Tube Heat Exchanger
→ Lab: Perform heat exchanger experiment
→ Subject: Heat Transfer
→ Calculators: LMTD / NTU / Heat Duty
→ Industry: Industrial heat exchanger
→ Control: Temperature control loop
→ Safety: Pressure/temperature hazards
→ Troubleshooting: Fouling / low heat-transfer performance
→ Viva: Heat exchanger questions
→ Career: Process/Design/Utilities Engineer
This interconnected architecture is more important than simply adding more content.
Build the Industry section so that adding a new industry later mostly means adding structured Industry → Process → Equipment → Data rather than writing new UI from scratch.
